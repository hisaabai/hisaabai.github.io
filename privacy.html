Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

HisaabAI is a personal expense and household-records app for Android, made by an individual developer ("HisaabAI", "we"). This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your phone. We do not have a server that receives it.
  • Backups go only to your own Google Drive, into a hidden app folder that only HisaabAI can read. We cannot see them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is saved in a database on your phone: expenses, income, credits, cards, EMI and chit funds, savings, budgets, trips, shopping and to-do lists, reminders, service history, documents and the photos you attach, health values and habits, and your profile (name, country, currency).

This data stays on the device. We do not collect it.

Google sign-in and Drive backup (optional)

  • If you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account email, name and profile photo. They are used only to show your account in the app and to make backups. They are stored on your phone.
  • When you tap Back up now, the app uploads one backup file (your database and attachments) to the app folder of your own Google Drive (Google's "appDataFolder"). Only HisaabAI can read this folder, using your sign-in. We have no access to it.
  • The app keeps your last 5 backups there and deletes older ones.
  • Backups are sent to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • You can use the app fully without signing in. In that case nothing leaves your phone.

Voice entry

  • When you use the microphone, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (provided by Google or your phone maker). That service may process the audio under its own privacy terms. HisaabAI does not receive, record or store the audio.
  • The app keeps a voice log on your phone (the recognized text, the alternatives the phone heard, and what was saved). It helps you check and improve voice entries. The voice log is never sent to us. It is included only in your own Drive backup.

Bank SMS paste

You can paste a bank message into the app to fill in an entry. The app reads only the text you paste, on your phone. The app does not have permission to read your SMS messages.

Health data

Health values and habits are stored only on your phone. They leave the phone only inside your own Drive backup, if you make one.

Deleting your data

  • On the phone: uninstall HisaabAI, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Backups: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → HisaabAI → "Delete hidden app data". Or sign out of HisaabAI and remove its access in your Google account (Security → Third-party apps).

Because we never hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

HisaabAI is meant for adults (18+) managing personal and household money.

Future AI features

Later versions may add optional online AI features (for example, a smarter voice assistant or bill scanning). They will be off until you choose to use them. Before they are released, this policy will be updated to say exactly what is sent, where, and why.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
