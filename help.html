Getting started

  • Home shows your modules as chats. Tap one to open it; long-press and drag to reorder.
  • The Hisaab icon at the top right of every screen takes you back to Home.
  • Turn modules on or off in Profile → Modules. Turned-off modules keep their data.
  • Amounts are in your base currency (Profile). Other currencies are saved with the rate of that day and never recalculated.

Try this: Open Profile → Modules and turn on only what you use.

Expenses

  • Tap + to add an expense: date, type, amount, how you paid (cash, card, bank).
  • The chips at the top are Quick options: one tap fills a whole entry (for example Tea 5). Add your own under Manage.
  • Long-press entries to select and delete them. Tap the search icon to search all months.
  • Swipe the month at the top to see older months.

Try this: Tap a quick option chip, then Save. A whole entry in two taps.

Voice entry

  • Tap Speak and say an expense, for example "Lulu grocery 45 by card".
  • The form opens filled in. Check it and press Save. Nothing is saved without Save.
  • If you correct the type, HisaabAI asks "Remember 'lulu' as Grocery?". Yes = next time it is filled in for you. No = it is never asked again.
  • Your phone's own speech service turns speech into text. HisaabAI never receives the audio.

Try this: Say "tea 5" and check how the form is filled.

Bank SMS paste

  • In your Messages app, share a bank SMS to HisaabAI, or copy it and use Paste bank SMS in Expenses.
  • The amount, currency, card, shop and date are read on your phone. The app has no SMS permission and reads only the text you give it.
  • The same SMS twice? HisaabAI tells you it was already added.
  • If you change the type before Save, HisaabAI asks "Remember this?" so the next SMS from that shop is filled in. A card picked by hand can remember its last 4 digits.

Try this: Copy one bank SMS and paste it into Expenses.

Savings & Goals

  • A pot holds cash or gold you put aside. Add money in or take it out.
  • A goal has a target amount and date; the pot shows how far you are.

Try this: Create a pot for an emergency fund and add the first amount.

Budgets

  • Set a monthly budget per type or for a group of types (for example Food = Grocery + Restaurant).
  • You get a note at 80% and at 100% of a budget.

Try this: Set a budget for your biggest type and watch it fill during the month.

Credits

  • Money you lent or borrowed, one chat per person.
  • Each entry shows the running balance. Share a reminder on WhatsApp from the chat.

Try this: Add a person and the money you lent them.

Credit Cards

  • Each card has its statements, due date, limit and payments.
  • Add the card's last 4 digits (card details) so bank SMS picks the right card.
  • A card payment is not an expense: the purchases already are.

Try this: Open a card and add its last 4 digits and due day.

EMI & Kuri

  • See what is paid, what is left and the next due date. Reminders come before each due.

Try this: Add a loan with its monthly amount and start date.

Service History

Try this: Add your vehicle's last service and set the next one.

Documents

Masters

  • Masters are your lists: expense types, subtypes, cards, people, vehicles.
  • Edit a master to rename it or add aliases (other words for it, used by voice and bank SMS). Remove an alias there too.
  • Merge into… moves everything from one master to another (for example Dress → Clothing). It cannot be undone.

Try this: Open Masters and add an alias you use, for example "lulu" for Grocery.

Backup & restore

  • Profile → Back up now saves your data to your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder only HisaabAI can read. The last 5 backups are kept.
  • New phone or reinstall: sign in with the same Google account and choose Restore.
  • Backups are manual. HisaabAI reminds you when the last backup is old.

Try this: Sign in and make your first backup.

Questions and answers

Does HisaabAI work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Only Google sign-in and Drive backup need internet.

Can HisaabAI see my money or my messages?

No. Your data is on your phone and in your own Drive backup. The app has no SMS permission: it reads only an SMS you share or paste.

I changed phones. How do I get my data back?

Install HisaabAI, sign in with the same Google account and choose Restore when it shows "Backup found".

Why was a type filled in wrongly from voice or SMS?

The app matches words to your masters. Correct it before Save and answer Yes to "Remember this?". You can also add or remove aliases in Masters.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app (or clear its storage). For backups: Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → HisaabAI → Delete hidden app data.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Profile → Help & Support → Feedback. It opens your email app with the app version filled in. Email: team.hisaabai@gmail.com
